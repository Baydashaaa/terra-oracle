Terra Oracle Classic

A hybrid Q&A and governance system: payments and reputation settle on Terra Classic, while question and answer text lives in a public Git-backed dataset. Every action has economic weight.
No login. Only wallet.

All transactions verifiable on Terra Classic blockchain

What is a question?

A question on Terra Oracle is not just text - it's an economic action on the blockchain. When you pay to ask - 50,000 LUNC for a Basic question or 200,000 for Priority - that fee is split automatically:

50%
→ Weekly Prize Pool
50%
→ Protocol Treasury

You also receive +40 REP and +1 free Draw entry (Basic) or +4 (Priority) - your contribution immediately returns value back to you. Oracle Draw entries also earn +10 REP per entry.

Pseudonymity · Web3 native
🕶
No login. No email. No account.

Your identity is your wallet address. It is shown as a short alias, but the full address is stored with everything you post and stays publicly readable - that is what lets anyone verify a payment or a vote. So you take part without revealing who you are, while everything done from one address can be linked together. This is a core Web3 principle: sovereignty over your own identity, not invisibility.

Reputation = real value

REP is not just points. It's your position in the protocol - and it directly affects how much LUNC you earn from the weekly pool.

Fee discount
Higher rank = lower cost per question. Up to −25% at Ascended rank.
Reward multiplier
Your weekly pool share is multiplied by your rank. Up to ×1.5 at Ascended - deliberately low, so newcomers can still catch up.
