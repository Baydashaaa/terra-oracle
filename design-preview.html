      CIRCUIT

      Claim consecutive zones on a 250‑zone board. A marker runs across it and stops on one - whoever owns that zone takes the round.

      How it works. Zones are handed out back to back from the first free one. A round draws when the board fills, or every three hours once at least 20 zones are taken. Below that the zones and the pot carry into the next round - nothing is refunded and nothing is lost.

      Price. 1,000 LUNC for your first zone, +5% for each one after it. The markup is cumulative per wallet per round, so splitting a purchase does not dodge it. Your Oracle Mask sets the cap: 10 zones for Common, 25 for Rare, 50 for Legendary.

      The pool splits 80% to the winner, 6% to TCO rewards for everyone who took zones, 6% to TCO burn, 8% to the treasury.

      Rewards. Your share is fixed in LUNC the moment a round draws, then converted to TCO in one batch once 50,000 LUNC has built up. Claims are cumulative.

      The draw is checkable. The winning zone comes from the Terra Classic block hash at the round deadline, not from a number our server picked.

      How the draw works Nobody picks the winner

      The result comes out of a Terra Classic block that did not exist yet when entries closed, and anyone can replay it afterwards.

      Step 1 · Entries

      Every Oracle Mask you mint adds entries to the round. The tier decides how many. More entries mean a wider slice, never a guarantee.

      Common · 1 entry Rare · 5 entries Legendary · 10 entries
      Step 2 · The block

      The draw uses the first block produced at or after 20:00 UTC of the round deadline, not whichever block happens to be latest when the script runs. Re-running the draw later cannot change the winner.

      Step 3 · The result

      The block hash and the frozen snapshot of entries pick the winner. The same two inputs always give the same answer, so anyone can repeat it.

      Verify a draw Pick a completed round

      Pick a round and your browser will run the steps above, then compare the outcome with what was recorded on chain.

        What is Oracle Reputation

        Oracle Reputation (REP) measures your contribution to the protocol. It is not a token and not a balance - it reflects activity, quality and engagement across every module. REP accumulates over time and unlocks ranks, fee discounts and weekly rewards.

        How REP is earned

        Anti-abuse limits

        • Answers are limited to 3 per question per day.
        • Answer REP is progressive per day: answers 1-5 earn +40, answers 6-10 earn +10, later ones earn 0. Only the REP stops, the answer still posts.
        • Upvotes grant REP only from a wallet with paid on-chain history.
        • Voting is capped at 20 votes per day per wallet.
        • Self-votes on your own questions and answers are blocked.

        Oracle ascension Each rank unlocks a fee discount and a reward multiplier

        Weekly rewards pool

        Each week 25% of protocol treasury income moves to the REP Rewards Pool wallet, and the full balance is paid out to the top 20% of contributors ranked by REP earned in the last 7 days.

        REWARD FORMULA

        Your share = (your 7-day REP × rank multiplier) divided by the total weighted REP of the top 20%, multiplied by the weekly pool.

        SCORING PERIOD

        Only the last 7 days count toward weekly rewards. All-time REP still decides your rank and your fee discount.

        ACTIVATION THRESHOLD Weekly rewards only activate when at least 10 unique contributors are active that week. Below that the pool carries over and keeps accumulating.

        REP persistence

        ALL-TIME REP

        Accumulates forever. Decides your rank and your fee discount. Never resets.

        WEEKLY REP

        Only the last 7 days. Resets each week. Used solely for reward distribution.
