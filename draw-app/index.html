CIRCUIT

Claim consecutive zones on a 250‑zone board. A marker runs across it and stops on one - whoever owns that zone takes the round.

How it works. Zones are handed out back to back from the first free one. A round draws when the board fills, or every three hours once at least 20 zones are taken. Below that the zones and the pot carry into the next round - nothing is refunded and nothing is lost.

Price. 1,000 LUNC for your first zone, +5% for each one after it. The markup is cumulative per wallet per round, so splitting a purchase does not dodge it. Your Oracle Mask sets the cap: 10 zones for Common, 25 for Rare, 50 for Legendary.

The pool splits 80% to the prize bucket, 6% to TCO rewards for everyone who took zones, 6% to the TCO burn side, 8% to the treasury.

Neighbours take a cut of the prize. By default 5% of the prize bucket is shared between the owners of zones immediately next to the winning one, when those zones belong to somebody else. The winner takes the rest - and the whole bucket when no eligible neighbour exists. The 5% is an operator setting, not a contract rule, so it can change; the amount actually paid is recorded in each round's snapshot.

The burn side is held, not yet burned. After the batch buyback that share of TCO is transferred to terra10zptfez4jdvakrhu58q4nqj2te7mnpewqhu27a and stays there. The irreversible burn is deferred until the token's whitelist allows it. Tokens sitting at that address are not the same as tokens destroyed, and the balance is public.

Rewards. Your share is fixed in LUNC the moment a round draws, then converted to TCO in one batch once 50,000 LUNC has built up - buying a few cents' worth per round would be eaten by fees. Claims are cumulative, so many epochs come out in a single transaction.

The draw is checkable. The winning zone comes from the Terra Classic block hash at the round's deadline, not from a number our server picked.

How the Draw Works

Nobody picks the winner. The result comes out of a Terra Classic block that did not exist yet when entries closed - and anyone can replay it afterwards.

Step 1 · Entries

Every Oracle Mask you mint adds entries to the round. The tier decides how many. More entries mean a wider slice, never a guarantee.

Common · 1 entry Rare · 5 entries Legendary · 10 entries

At draw time the whole list is frozen into a public snapshot - that file is what makes the result checkable later. Free entries earned on Terra Oracle count too: they are tracked in free-entries.json, committed hourly, and sent into the pool contract before the round closes. The contract refuses them afterwards, so nothing can be added once the outcome is knowable.

Step 2 · The block

The draw uses the first block produced at or after 20:00 UTC of the round deadline - not whichever block happens to be latest when the script runs. That block is fixed by the schedule, so re-running the draw later cannot change the winner, and nobody can wait for a hash they like. Validators produce it independently; no one involved in the draw can influence it.

Step 3 · The winner

The two draws use different rules, because Weekly has to pick several different people.

Daily · one winner
index = BigInt("0x" + block_hash) % total_entries

The hash read as one huge number, divided by the entry count. The remainder is the winning entry.

Weekly · up to three places
seed = block_hash
for place p in 0, 1, 2:
  seed  = sha256(seed + p)
  index = BigInt("0x" + seed) % total_entries
  while that wallet already won:
    index = index + 1

Each place is seeded from the previous one, so the chain must be replayed in order. If a place lands on a wallet that already won, the index steps forward until it reaches a new one - one wallet cannot take two places. With fewer than three participants, fewer places are paid and the unclaimed share stays in the pool.

Pick a round below and your browser will run exactly these steps, then compare the outcome with what was recorded on chain.
